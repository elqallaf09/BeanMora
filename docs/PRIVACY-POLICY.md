# BeanMora Privacy Policy / سياسة خصوصية BeanMora

Effective date / تاريخ السريان: 24 October 2026 / 24 أكتوبر 2026

## English

BeanMora is operated by Mohammed AlQallaf. For privacy, account, support or content-report requests, contact **Beanmora.app@gmail.com**. This policy covers BeanMora's coffee catalog, account, saved content, brewing and community features. Feature availability depends on your installed version.

### Information processed

You can browse the public coffee catalog without an account. When you create or use an account, BeanMora processes your email address, account ID, username and profile information you choose to provide, such as your name, biography and profile photo. Where your installed version offers country or phone fields, these are processed only when supplied. Your selected public profile information can be visible to other members; account email and phone are not published in the public profile.

When you use member features, BeanMora stores the content you submit or save: coffee inventory, equipment, recipes, favorites, preparation settings, tasting notes and brew history. Community features process posts, comments, likes, follow relationships, reports and photos or other media that you choose to upload. Versions offering private messages, video or voice recording also process the messages and media you choose to send. Free-form content should not contain sensitive personal information.

The authentication and hosting providers process technical request and security information, including authentication events, timestamps, IP addresses and client information, to operate and protect the service. An account identifier may link these records to your account.

### How information is used

We use information to authenticate accounts, synchronize saved content, provide requested social and coffee features, personalize coffee suggestions using your preferences and brewing history, respond to support requests, moderate reported content, detect misuse and diagnose service problems.

BeanMora does not include an advertising network or an advertising-tracking system. We do not use app information to track you across other companies' apps and websites for targeted advertising or share it with data brokers.

### Service providers and public content

Supabase provides authentication, database and storage services. Application distribution and hosting providers process information needed to deliver the service. If you choose a social sign-in provider, such as Google or Apple when offered, that provider processes the sign-in under its own privacy policy and provides the account information authorized by you. These services may process information outside your country.

Content that you publish publicly may be displayed with your public profile. Choose visibility carefully. Follow relationships are used within BeanMora; the app does not upload your device address book. Third-party websites, product links and videos opened from the app have their own privacy practices.

### Device storage and permissions

BeanMora caches language preferences, catalog information and saved content on your device. Native account sessions use protected system storage. Photo, camera or microphone access is requested when the relevant feature needs it; you can decline or change permission in device settings. The app does not request precise device location for its coffee features. Country information, where offered, is selected by the user.

### Retention and deletion

Account content is retained to provide your account and the features you choose. You can edit or delete supported content and request account deletion from **My account** or **Settings > Account**, depending on the installed version. Confirm the in-app deletion request and wait for completion; a network interruption may require another attempt. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account.

Deletion removes active account data and associated uploaded files through the service's deletion process. Limited security records and provider backups can remain for security, legal or backup purposes according to the provider's retention lifecycle. Cached public content or previously shared copies may remain on other devices until refreshed or removed. Some releases automatically expire private messages after 24 hours; expiry is not a promise of immediate deletion from backups or recipients' copies.

For help with access, correction or deletion, email **Beanmora.app@gmail.com** from the account address where possible. Do not send your password or verification codes. We may need to verify account ownership before completing a request.

### Updates

We update this policy when the service's data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version. Material changes will be reflected in the policy and applicable app privacy disclosures.

عند استخدام ميزات الأعضاء، نخزّن ما تضيفه أو تحفظه من مخزون البن والمعدات والوصفات والمفضلة وإعدادات التحضير وملاحظات التذوق وسجل التحضير. تعالج ميزات المجتمع المنشورات والتعليقات والإعجابات وعلاقات المتابعة والبلاغات والصور والوسائط التي تختار رفعها. وفي الإصدارات التي تتيح الرسائل الخاصة أو الفيديو أو التسجيل الصوتي، نعالج الرسائل والوسائط التي تختار إرسالها. تجنّب إدخال معلومات شخصية حساسة في النصوص الحرة.

يعالج مقدمو خدمات المصادقة والاستضافة معلومات الطلبات والسجلات التقنية والأمنية، ومنها أحداث تسجيل الدخول والتوقيت وعنوان الإنترنت ومعلومات العميل، لتشغيل الخدمة وحمايتها. قد ترتبط هذه السجلات بحسابك عبر معرّفه.

### الاحتفاظ والحذف

نحتفظ بمحتوى الحساب لتشغيل الحساب والميزات التي تختارها. يمكنك تعديل المحتوى أو حذفه حيث تتيح الميزة ذلك، وطلب حذف الحساب من «حسابي» أو «الإعدادات > الحساب» بحسب الإصدار. أكّد الحذف وانتظر اكتماله؛ وقد يستلزم انقطاع الشبكة إعادة المحاولة. حذف التطبيق وحده لا يحذف الحساب.

تزيل عملية الحذف بيانات الحساب النشطة وملفاته المرتبطة. قد تبقى سجلات أمنية محدودة ونسخ احتياطية لدى مقدم الخدمة لأغراض أمنية أو نظامية أو وفق دورة النسخ الاحتياطية. وقد تبقى نسخ من المحتوى العام أو المشارَك على أجهزة أخرى إلى أن تُحدّث أو تُحذف. تنتهي الرسائل الخاصة تلقائيًا بعد 24 ساعة في بعض الإصدارات؛ ولا يعني ذلك الحذف الفوري من النسخ الاحتياطية أو نسخ المستلمين.

لطلب المساعدة في الوصول إلى البيانات أو تصحيحها أو حذفها، راسل **Beanmora.app@gmail.com**، ويُفضّل من البريد المرتبط بالحساب. لا ترسل كلمة المرور أو رموز التحقق. قد نحتاج إلى التحقق من ملكية الحساب قبل تنفيذ الطلب.
