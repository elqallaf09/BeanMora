# BeanMora 0.5.21 — selected tab menu and coffee knowledge

Prepared source release. Local Android versionCode is 32 and iOS buildNumber is 14; EAS increments native build numbers remotely. These changes require a new installed build. A Metro export is not a signed APK or an iOS archive.

## Resulting behavior

- The user's selected menu mockup 7 is now a compact underline tab rail. Five primary entries lead to coffee, recipes, equipment, roasteries and More; existing secondary destinations remain in More. The selected Arabic tab scrolls into view at narrow widths. Appearance and language use the same equal-width underline controls in Settings.
- The redundant Beans / Recipes / For you and All coffee / Complete personality buttons are removed from the coffee list. Partial reviewed profiles remain discoverable and retain their honest source metadata. All fifteen brewing methods remain accessible through a searchable All methods menu plus a short rail of popular methods.
- Bottom navigation is Home, Discover, coffeeHO, Brew, Account, placing coffeeHO in the center. Settings retains account security, sign-out and deletion; these controls remain absent from My account.
- Roast Lab opens a guest-accessible learning page with an explicitly illustrative green/light/medium/dark bean image, descriptions of each stage, how to reach it and taste tendencies. Eleven expandable sourced chapters explain green coffee, stages, Maillard reactions, development, energy/airflow, RoR, color measurement, defects, cooling, resting and cupping. Existing batch logs, green stock and community roasts remain available from the lab tabs.
- The complete Arabic study source is `docs/roasting-study.ar.html`, exported as a separate ten-page PDF. It explains a repeatable experiment, measurement calculations, blind tasting and a blank recording template. It does not claim that the experiment was performed or prescribe one universal temperature/DTR for all roasters.
- Equipment details now offer model-specific thumbnails, image selection, a full-size modal and photo provenance. The reviewed gallery data contains 383 verified photographs for 109 of 126 reviewed models, with two to four images per model. Seventeen models retain their existing approved cover because additional exact-model images could not be verified. Galleries include same-model color variants; mixed-model comparisons, video thumbnails and unrelated promotions are excluded. Images remain at their publisher and are marked `source_linked`, not licensed-for-redistribution.
- The free on-device coffee expert now has 44 bilingual sourced lessons in seven domains, plus the existing fifteen method guides, arithmetic, troubleshooting and catalog retrieval. A searchable knowledge library and substantive follow-up explanations keep the page compact. Manufacturer-specific grinder and water values stay identified as such. Budget/currency queries still go through catalog evidence instead of matching generic equipment words. This expands the existing rules/knowledge system; it does not train a general-purpose language model or add a paid inference provider.
